# Mitudom Kindergarten — Product

## Register
brand

## Platform
web

## Purpose
Help parents considering a kindergarten understand the school's approach, build confidence, and enter the enrollment funnel. The primary action is **นัดเยี่ยมชมโรงเรียน**; the secondary campaign action is **ลงทะเบียน Open House 28 ตุลาคม 2569**.

## Audience and positioning
Thai-speaking parents researching an early-years school. The experience should show a happy childhood, attentive care, safety, and readiness for the next step. Avoid claims about facilities, curricula, outcomes, fees, or admissions processes until the school supplies evidence.

## Experience principles
- Lead with children and the lived school experience.
- Tell a connected story with photography and clear Thai language.
- Make the visit action easy to find throughout the page.
- Keep mobile navigation and reading effortless.
- Treat approved headlines and supplied facts as source of truth.

## Scope
First iteration: complete homepage only, navigation linking to homepage sections, footer, responsive structure, image slots, metadata, and quality checks. Future pages may cover Our Approach, Programs, Why Mitudom, Life at Mitudom, Admissions, Open House, and Contact after homepage approval. Their detailed content awaits approval and source material.

## Brand character and exclusions
Modern, warm, trustworthy, child-centered, calm, thoughtful, premium but approachable. Avoid SaaS, corporate, luxury international-school, cartoon kindergarten, generic AI, and marketplace-template aesthetics.

## Accessibility
Semantic HTML, keyboard-accessible navigation and links, readable contrast, responsive layout, and reduced-motion support.
